# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want SealedCash to be a welcoming, technically rigorous, and privacy-conscious project. Participants should contribute with respect, assume good faith, and focus criticism on ideas and code.

## Expected behavior

- Use inclusive and professional language.
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence.
- Protect private information and never request credentials in public.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about security, scope, and release readiness.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate exposure of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [support@sealedcash.com](mailto:support@sealedcash.com). Maintainers may remove content, pause participation, or restrict access when necessary to protect the community.
